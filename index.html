Welcome to Sketch-A-Etch!

How to play

First you have to define how big you want your board to be.

For that, choose appropriate Dimensions in the height- and width inputboxes. I suggest < 100 each due to performance issues.

Then you should add the rows first, then columns (whether Start or End matters not).

Once the board is created, you can press on the screen, and sketch-your-etch!

Change resize factor and brush color
